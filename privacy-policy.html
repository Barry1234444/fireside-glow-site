Privacy Policy — Fireside Glow

Fireside Glow: Fireplace TV · Version 1.0.0 · Effective November 7, 2026

1. What we collect

We collect nothing.

Fireside Glow does not require an account or login, and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts, device identifier, advertising identifier, usage data, or any other personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or push-notification SDKs.

2. How the app works

The app has one job: play ambient theme videos on your TV.

  • From the home screen, tap a theme — for example a fireplace, Christmas, or New Year scene — and it plays full screen, with sound.
  • While a video is playing you can mute the sound, and you can set a sleep timer.
  • In Settings you can also choose whether sound is on or off by default when a video starts.
  • When the sleep timer ends, the video stops and the app shows a Good Night screen.

That is the whole app. There is no login, no social feature, no chat, no user-generated content, and no cloud sync.

3. What stays on your Apple TV only

The app stores only your own playback preferences on this Apple TV, for example whether sound is muted, the default sound setting in Settings (on or off), and the sleep timer you set. These settings never leave the device, are never uploaded or synced anywhere, and are deleted when you delete the app.

4. Purchases

Fireside Glow is a one-time purchase. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information, and buying the app does not require an account with us.

5. Permissions and third-party services

The app does not request any system permissions (no camera, microphone, photos, location, or notifications). Version 1.0 has no third-party SDKs, no embedded web pages, and no links that send data out of the app. If a future version adds a third-party service, a permission, advertising, or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Children

Fireside Glow is a family-friendly ambient video app. It contains no social features, no chat, and no user-generated content. Children should use it with a parent or guardian nearby, as with any TV app.

7. Changes to this policy and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same public URL and the date above will be updated. The in-app copy (Settings > Privacy Policy) will be updated to match. The in-app copy is stored locally in the app.
